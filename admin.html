    Site-wide banner

    Shows a scrolling banner on every page — student and staff, signed in or not — until you clear it. Use this for things like "the system is down for maintenance."

    Choose a preset above to fill this in, or pick "Custom message…" and write your own.

    Lockdown mode

    Blocks sign-in for everyone except admins — students and staff alike. Anyone currently signed in sees the banner as a warning, and gets signed out and sent back to the sign-in page the next time the app checks (within a few seconds, or on their next page load). Turn it off to let everyone back in immediately.

    Choose a preset above to fill this in, or pick "Custom message…" and write your own.
